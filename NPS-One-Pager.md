# Understanding NPS (Net Promoter Score)

## What is NPS?

NPS measures customer loyalty based on one simple question:

> *"How likely are you to recommend us to a friend or colleague?"*

Customers rate their likelihood on a scale of 0-10.

---

## Customer Categories

| Category | Rating | What it means |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Promoters** | 9-10 | Loyal enthusiasts who will keep coming back and refer others |
| **Passives** | 7-8 | Satisfied but unenthusiastic — vulnerable to competitor offers |
| **Detractors** | 1-6 | Unhappy customers who can damage your brand through negative word-of-mouth |

---

## How NPS is Calculated

```
NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors
```

---

## NPS Score Ranges

| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| **+100 to +50** | Excellent |
| **+49 to +30** | Good |
| **+29 to +0** | Needs Improvement |
| **Below 0** | Poor (more detractors than promoters) |

---

## Example

If you receive:
- 50% Promoters (9-10)
- 30% Passives (7-8)
- 20% Detractors (1-6)

**NPS = 50 − 20 = +30** (Good)

---

## Why NPS Matters

- **Predicts business growth** — Promoters drive referrals
- **Identifies pain points** — Detractors reveal what needs fixing
- **Tracks over time** — Measure if improvements are working
- **Compares to competitors** — Industry benchmarks available
